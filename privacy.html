The short version

Your accounting data never reaches us. AK Smarts Books is software that runs on your own computer and works offline day to day. Your invoices, ledgers, customer and employee details, tax figures and backups are created and stored on your device. They are never sent to AK Smarts and we have no way to see them.

What we do and do not collect

  • Your books: stay on your computer, or in a backup file you save to a USB drive or your own Google Drive or OneDrive folder. We never receive them.
  • Sign-up details: the name, email, phone and location you enter when you start the free trial are saved only in the app on your computer. They are not sent to us.
  • Gmail (payslips): if you choose to email payslips, they are sent from your own Gmail account. We never see the recipients or the content.
  • Licence activation: to issue or renew a licence we use the installation ID you send us (a random code for your computer) and the details you give us, such as your name and email. We keep these only to manage your licence.
  • Licence check: about once a day, when online, the app sends our licence server your licence key ID, your installation ID, the app version and the number of companies you keep in the app (a count only, never their names), so we can tell whether this computer is the one currently licensed. No accounting data, names or books are sent. If the check cannot be made for longer than your offline period (7 days unless agreed otherwise), or the licence has been moved to another computer or suspended, the app becomes read-only: you can still view, export and back up your books.
  • Tax-rule updates: when online, AK Smarts Books may check our website for updated tax rates. This is an ordinary file download and sends none of your data.

Cookies and tracking

The AK Smarts Books app uses your browser's local storage only to keep your own settings and books on your device. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

Your rights

Because your accounting data stays on your device, you are in full control of it at all times. For the limited licence details we hold, you may ask us to correct or delete them by contacting us.

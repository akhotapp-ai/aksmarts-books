AK Smarts Books

Your books. Your computer. Nobody else.

Powerful offline accounting that runs fully on your own computer — your books never touch the internet, never sit on anyone’s server, and stay yours alone.

Invoices, purchases, bank reconciliation, GST and VAT returns, TDS, payroll and reports. Fast, private and offline.

Open

Install it like a desktop app: in Chrome or Edge, click the install icon at the right end of the address bar, or open the browser menu and choose Install AK Smarts Books. It then opens in its own window from the Start menu, Dock or desktop and works offline.

1. Install

Click Install AK Smarts Books. It gets its own icon and window, like any desktop app, and works without internet.

2. Open it

Open AK Smarts Books from the Start menu, Dock or desktop icon. You get a 30-day free trial.

3. Activate

Send us the installation ID shown under Setup, Licence. We send back a licence key made for your computer. Paste it there. No internet needed.

Good to know
  1. Your accounts never come to us. You choose where they are saved: a file on your computer or your own Google Drive. Use Back up your data regularly.
  2. Tax rates and due dates update automatically when you are online.
  3. AK Smarts Books works fully offline. Each licence key works only on the computer it was made for. Changing computers? Ask us for a new key.
